SmartCart AI image collection checklist

For EACH class, collect images across these condition groups:
- angles: front view back view side view top view tilted 30 degree tilted 45 degree
- distances: close up 15 cm medium distance 40 cm far view 70 cm
- lighting: daylight indoor fluorescent lighting dim light shadows backlit
- backgrounds: plain white background wooden table supermarket shelf shopping cart basket handheld
- occlusion: partially occluded partly hidden behind another product partially out of frame
- multiple_items: multiple products two three same items mixed grocery products together

Suggested target: 100-150 usable images per class. The 6 query groups are broad; they do not replace a manual check that front/back/side/top, close/medium/far, lighting, backgrounds, occlusion, and multiple-item scenarios are actually represented.
For branded products, ensure the package identity is visible. For fresh produce, capture different specimens, sizes, colors, and orientations.
Keep original images where possible. Do not create synthetic condition variants by rotating or editing one image and count them as independent real photographs.
